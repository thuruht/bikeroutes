Why the roundabout wheel. It reads instantly as a bike wheel (hub + rim) while the route thread entering and exiting makes it a navigation mark, not just a cycle — a roundabout every cyclist knows. It holds up at 16 px, works as a single-color glyph, and the orange exit-node ties straight to the destination pin on the map.
