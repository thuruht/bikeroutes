Design System

The single source of truth for bikeroutes.org — a rugged, map-first tool for cyclists. Camo-inspired surfaces, blaze-orange focus, and Reki the deer for warmth. Every token here is defined once in tokens.css and consumed by the live product.

MO Camo Edition Outfit · IBM Plex Light + Dark WCAG AA Mobile-first

01 Color

Eight fixed brand colors form the palette. The UI never references a raw hex — it uses theme-aware semantic tokens that map onto the brand colors, occasionally AA-tuned for contrast on a given surface.

02 Typography

Two families do the work: Outfit (geometric, modern) for headers and the wordmark; IBM Plex Sans (highly legible) for UI and trail data. IBM Plex Mono carries coordinates, captions, and data readouts. No aggressive all-caps body — legibility wins on a glaring phone screen outdoors.

06 Reki — Voice & Usage

Reki is a yearling white-tailed buck — curious, adventurous, friendly. He's a tool for empathy and brand warmth, never condescending. Use him for the human moments; keep him out of the core routing workflow.

Brand wink · why the winged cap
Reki nods to Mercury (Greek Hermes) — the wing-capped god of roads, travelers, and messengers, the patron of everyone moving between places. His petasos wings flank the cap's navigation diamond, so the emblem reads two ways at once: a heading diamond and the god of the road. Antlers say deer; wings say go.
Use Reki for
  • Empty states & no-results
  • 404 / error pages
  • Loading screens
  • Milestones & donation appeals

07 Accessibility

Built for one-handed, gloved, glaring-sun use on a trail. The system bakes these in so screens inherit them by default.

✓Visible focus. Every interactive element shows a 2px blaze --focus-ring on keyboard focus (:focus-visible) — never outline:none with no replacement.
✓Touch floors. Controls are ≥44px on coarse pointers (--touch-min).
✓AA contrast. Body text and accents tuned per theme to clear 4.5:1 on their surface.
✓Reduced motion. All non-essential animation collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.
✓State, not just color. Errors pair aria-invalid + an icon + text, not red alone.
✓Color-scheme aware. Native controls follow the theme via color-scheme.
